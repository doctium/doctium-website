## When telemedicine is the right choice — and when it is not

Being responsible about online care means being honest about its limits.

**Telemedicine is well suited to:**

- Follow-up visits and chronic-condition monitoring
- Medication reviews, repeat prescriptions, and dosage questions
- Minor, non-urgent symptoms and general health advice
- Getting a second opinion or deciding whether you need to be seen in person

**You should seek in-person or emergency care for:**

- Chest pain, difficulty breathing, or signs of stroke (sudden weakness, facial droop, slurred speech)
- Severe bleeding, major injury, or loss of consciousness
- Severe abdominal pain, a high fever that will not settle, or a fast-deteriorating child
- Anything that feels like a true emergency

If you are ever unsure, treat it as urgent and go to the nearest facility. Leenah is designed to push you toward in-person care when your symptoms warrant it, but your own judgement and the nearest emergency service always come first.
