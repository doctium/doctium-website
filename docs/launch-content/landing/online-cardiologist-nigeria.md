## When a video consult with an online heart doctor makes sense

A virtual visit is well suited to planning, monitoring and follow-up. You can reasonably use an online heart doctor for:

- Reviewing high or fluctuating blood pressure readings and adjusting a management plan
- Discussing palpitations, mild breathlessness on exertion or swelling that has been building over days
- Going over an ECG, echocardiogram report or lipid panel you already have
- Medication reviews, refills and questions about side effects
- Ongoing management of hypertension, heart failure or high cholesterol
- A second opinion before a procedure, or guidance on which in-person tests to arrange

Because a cardiologist often needs objective data, a video consult works best when you bring recent measurements: home blood pressure readings, a pulse rate, weight, and any reports or photos of prior results. The clearer your picture, the more useful the plan.

## When to choose in-person or emergency care instead

Telemedicine is not for emergencies. Chest pain is the clearest example. If you have chest pain or pressure, especially pain spreading to the arm, jaw or back, severe shortness of breath, fainting, a very fast or irregular heartbeat with dizziness, or weakness or slurred speech, treat it as an emergency and go to the nearest hospital straight away. Do not wait for an online appointment.

A virtual consult also cannot replace hands-on examination or tests that must be done in a facility, such as a 12-lead ECG, echocardiogram, stress test or blood draw. In these cases your online cardiologist can tell you what to arrange and where, then review the results with you afterwards.
