## What an online paediatric consultation covers

Many common childhood concerns are well suited to an online paediatric consultation, especially for assessment, advice, and follow-up. A paediatrician can help with concerns such as:

- Fevers, colds, coughs, and sore throats
- Skin rashes, eczema flare-ups, and minor allergic reactions
- Feeding, nutrition, and growth questions for infants and toddlers
- Mild diarrhoea or vomiting and home rehydration guidance
- Reviewing existing prescriptions, test results, and follow-up after a clinic visit
- General questions about immunisation schedules and child development

The doctor will ask about symptoms, how long they have lasted, your child's age and weight, and any existing conditions. Where it helps, you can show a rash or a swelling on camera. The paediatrician makes the clinical decision; the platform supports the conversation but never replaces the doctor's judgement.

## When to choose in-person or emergency care for children

Children, especially babies and toddlers, can become unwell quickly, and some situations are not suitable for a video visit. Telemedicine is not for emergencies. Take your child to the nearest hospital or emergency service straight away if you notice signs such as:

- Difficulty breathing, fast breathing, or bluish lips
- A seizure, unusual drowsiness, or being very hard to wake
- A stiff neck, a rash that does not fade when pressed, or a high fever in a baby under three months
- Persistent vomiting, signs of dehydration, or refusing all fluids
- A serious injury, deep wound, or suspected poisoning

An online paediatrician can help you judge how urgent a situation is, but when in doubt about a child, seek in-person care. The doctors on Doctium will tell you clearly when a physical examination or hospital visit is the right next step.
